# Smart Student Budget & Recipe Planner

Omar Kadan and Obida Jbara · Software Development Methods

## What our app does

Our proposed planner helps a student find three different dinners for one person within a grocery budget. It adds up what the student needs for all three dinners, subtracts what they already have, and prices the rest in whole packages. The plan includes the three meals, one shopping list, the estimated total, and the money left in the budget.

## Who uses it

Our app is for college students who cook for themselves and want to know what they can afford before shopping. A student enters their grocery budget and what they already have at home. They can enter everything by hand or use a photo to get ingredient suggestions. They check those suggestions and enter the amounts before asking for a meal plan.

## Current state

Our project idea has been approved, and we have a working Python prototype. We are using photo recognition to make it easier for students to enter what they have at home. We are checking how well it identifies ingredients in test photos. We are also preparing the proposal, requirements, and system design for the midterm.
